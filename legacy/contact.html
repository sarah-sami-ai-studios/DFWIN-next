FAQ

Common questions before a first call.

How fast can DFWin respond to a quote request?

Our standard service-level is a reply within one business day. A structured quote with landed-cost and lead time is typically back within 48 hours for catalogued solutions, longer for custom fabrication.

Do you work with new customers on small volumes?

Yes. Many of our most successful programs started with a single cabin-equipment category or one dry-dock window. We'll be straight with you if the economics don't work.

Can you ship direct to ship-side?

Yes. Through our bonded warehouses in Miami and Dallas we coordinate pier-side delivery and dry-dock kits across North America and European repositioning ports.

What classification society documentation do you support?

DNV, Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas, ABS, RINA and CCS. We coordinate type-approval paperwork with the supplier where required.

How do you handle quality control?

Every program is covered by inline production QA plus pre-shipment inspection using AQL sampling (ANSI/ASQ Z1.4). First-article inspection and CAPA processes are standard on new parts.

Are you a manufacturer or a trading company?

Neither. DFWin is a supply chain services company. We curate and audit an independent panel of manufacturers and bring quality, logistics and governance together for you.
