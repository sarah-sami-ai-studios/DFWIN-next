Solutions

Equipment & systems, specified for the world's most demanding operations.

Every DFWin solution is built on three pillars: marine-grade engineering, supplier quality we audit ourselves, and a bonded logistics network that delivers to ship-side or hotel door. Explore the three solution families below.

01 — Hospitality & Hotel

Cabin & guest-room equipment that survives 24/7 service.

From silent minibar fridges to luggage cages and housekeeping carts, we specify and source equipment that meets marine-grade durability, guest-experience standards and energy-efficiency targets — without premium-brand markup.

  • Silent minibar fridges — thermoelectric & compressor models, marine cabinets
  • Housekeeping & luggage carts — stainless, reinforced for shipboard traffic
  • Luggage cages — custom footprints, stacking and tracking options
  • In-room safes & hair-dryers — guest-experience approved
  • Operational consumables — amenities, linens, uniforms, cleaning systems
Scope this solution
02 — Marine Engineering

Technical components engineered for salt, vibration and class rules.

For newbuilds, refurbs and dry-dock windows where the schedule is unforgiving, we deliver engineered parts, fabrication and kits with the documentation classification societies expect.

  • Stainless & corrosion-resistant hardware — 316/316L spec standard
  • Custom fabrication — enclosures, brackets, piping assemblies
  • Dry-dock kits — pre-assembled to minimize yard time
  • Class-society documentation — DNV, LR, BV, ABS where required
  • Spare parts & logistics — scheduled and urgent replenishment
Scope this solution
03 — RFID & Smart Systems

Proximity access & smart beverage — measurable guest and margin impact.

RFID cards, bracelets and smart dispensers that tighten access control, cut shrinkage on refillable beverage programs and lift the measurable guest experience. Proven across cruise fleets and flagship hotel brands.

  • RFID access control — cabins, crew zones, back-of-house
  • RFID smart beverage systems — refillable bottles, guest-linked pour
  • Inventory & asset tagging — linens, F&B, technical stores
  • Integration support — PMS/POS handoff, reporting APIs
  • Cards, wristbands & readers — in-house spec or co-branded
