About us

Built by supply chain veterans, for industries that can't afford to stop.

Hong Kong DFWin Int'l Co. Limited was established in 2013 by a team of seasoned supply chain professionals. We exist to be the partner that owns the outcome — from supplier audit to ship-side delivery — for customers in North America and Europe.

Our story

Close to the supply base. Close to the outcome.

Our founders have diversified experiences in strategic sourcing, demand and planning, inventory and logistics management, manufacturing and project management. That breadth lets us see the whole picture — and act on it.

Operating in China, DFWin has the advantage of being close to the supply base. We understand the business environment, have rich experience working with both multinational companies and indigenous suppliers, and bring a strong local social network plus bilingual capability. That's how we serve you faster, better and at a better landed cost.

Our mission

Two commitments, in priority order.

  1. Become your trustworthy business partner. Trust compounds. We act like it's the only asset worth protecting.
  2. Enable your supply chain transformation. Not just ship product — reshape how you source, plan and deliver.
What we stand for

Four principles we apply, every week.

Ownership

One accountable partner across sourcing, quality and logistics. No finger-pointing between silos.

Transparency

Open landed-cost models, visible supplier panels, and honest conversations about risk and trade-offs.

Discipline

AQL-based inspection, class-society documentation, governance reviews — every program, every time.

Speed

On the ground in China, bonded in the US, bilingual, 24/7. Problems get answers the same day.
